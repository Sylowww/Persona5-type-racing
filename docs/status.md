# Status

Last updated: 2026-10-26.

## Built

- Bilingual (`/fr`, `/en`) home page with the Persona 5 theme: header, a full-screen red hero (shattered-glass portrait collage, slogan) that rolls up like a scroll as the player scrolls (only when the site opens on the home page), a cut-out-letter calling card title, a Persona 5–style main menu (Start a race with the Enter/Space shortcut, Quick play, Velvet Room, Find a lobby; the selected entry gets a red shard and a briefing box), the player's thief next to it (opens their character file), two crossing slogan bands, join-with-code form, typing preview, player dossier (stats, skill radar), footer.
- Skill radar on the home dossier (`skillRadar` in `lib/radar.ts`), from the last 20 races (saved races for accounts, the tab's races for guests): top speed (best WPM), average speed, accuracy (80% at the center, 100% at the edge), stamina (share of races finished), placement (average standing) and consistency (WPM spread between races; neutral after one race). Speed axes are full at 150 WPM. "Sync" is the average of the six axes. These scales are first guesses.
- Real-time multiplayer races (see ARCHITECTURE.md, Multiplayer). Signed-in players create a lobby ("Start a race" on home), others join with its 6-character code (or its URL), everyone readies up, the host starts, a synchronized 3 s countdown runs, all racers type the same text and see each other move live, and the server ends the race and sends everyone to the real results. Server-authoritative, in memory, single Node instance.
- Lobby page (`/{locale}/lobby/{code}`): player cards show the portrait of each player's character, live roster with connection status, ready meter, ready/unready, host-only start, leave (the action bar sits right under the lobby header). Capacity 30 by default (max 60, `LOBBY_CAPACITY`); free seats collapse into one invite card.
- Race page (`/{locale}/lobby/{code}/race`): countdown overlay, timer, live place from the server, race track where every racer is an animated runner (idle, run, jump over decorative obstacles, victory; dimmed while disconnected), typing arena, WPM, streak and accuracy, all inside a collapsing-palace atmosphere (cracks, debris, rumbles, alarm vignette, total chaos once the leader is halfway, a calling card and a Mona cut-in during the countdown, Mona's live comments, overtake flashes, stumbling runners, a glowing exit, a finish frame and a winner cut-in, final collapse before the results, results reveal, synthesized sound effects, Persona-style typing feats ("MISS", "TECHNICAL!", "CRITICAL", "1 MORE!"), the current word in cut-out letters, a "HOLD UP!" cut-in when passing a racer and a Third Eye toggle that races the player's record as a ghost; training races and their results are themed as the Velvet Room; see design-system.md). With powers on, an Ambush takes a word off the first clean first word, and the last racer can send one calling card per race that hides the leader's next words. Typing logic lives in `lib/typing.ts` and runs on both client and server.
- Results page (`/{locale}/lobby/{code}/results`): opening wedges and a Mona cut-in (congratulations, scolding for last place, or "go train"), banner, podium, speed chart, precision stats, slow keys and keyboard heatmap from the real race; back-to-lobby and rematch return to the same lobby.
- Race replay: results (right after a race and from the history) include a replay of the race on its track: runners move from each racer's saved progress samples (one per second, interpolated), spells mark the lanes they hit, and a log lists spells, calling cards and the Ambush; play/pause, restart, seek and ×1/×2/×4 speed (`lib/race-replay.ts`, `features/results/components/race-replay.tsx`). Races saved before replays existed have no progress samples or events, so they show no replay. Keystrokes are not replayed, only positions and events.
- Confidant ranks: every saved race raises the registered player's bond with the thief they raced as (rank 1 after the first race, max rank 10 after 46; `lib/confidants.ts`, counted from the saved results by `getRacesByCharacter`, so races saved before full results were stored do not count). A race that ranks a bond up shows a "RANK UP!" tarot card on its results, and each thief's character file shows the arcana card, rank pips and races left. The thresholds and the arcana of Black Mask (Justice, shared with Crow) and Sophia (Star) are first guesses.
- Bots: the host adds bots of any difficulty (novice ~30, rookie ~60, master ~120, godspeed ~150 WPM), each with its own level, and removes them in the lobby. They race with the same rules as players, with a human-like rhythm and typos they correct (see ARCHITECTURE.md, Bots). One player plus bots is enough to start, and so are bots alone (the host can watch as a spectator).
- Background music (`components/layout/music-player.tsx`, tracks in `public/music/`): the race page plays the race theme from 25 s (vocals start as the 3 s countdown ends), every other page the home theme chosen in the header (Theme I or II). Header buttons pick the theme and mute (the mute also silences sound effects); both choices are saved in `localStorage`. Playback starts on the first click or key press when the browser blocks autoplay.
- Race settings: the host picks the text language (French or English, defaults to the host's interface language), the mode (normal, forced fix, free or sudden death), powers on/off (catch-up bonuses), time limit (30 s to 10 min, or none), text type (real passages or random words), length, complexity, punctuation, capitals, accents, numbers, letter filters and case sensitivity in the settings panel (opened by "Lobby settings" or the rules summary's Edit button); every player sees the chosen rules live in the rules summary at the top of the right column, above the chat. The race page shows the time left for timed races and eliminates a racer at their first mistake in sudden death.
- Lobby chat: messages and quick taunts, shared live with every member (last 50, in memory with the lobby). The "Lobby settings" and "Chat / taunts" buttons jump to the rules and the chat input.
- Reconnection: 30 s grace period for a lost connection in the lobby and during a race; reloading the race page restores progress.

- Home menu: "Quick play // 1v1" opens matchmaking (another player, or a bot after 15 s; 30 s race, saved like any race), then a full-screen versus splash (both characters, names, "VS") before the race and "Velvet Room" (the training dojo) starts a solo practice race at once (not saved). "Start a race" still creates a custom lobby.
- Lobby browser: a custom lobby is joinable with its code by default; its host can make it public (or private) from the settings panel. The "Find a lobby" home card opens `/lobbies`, which lists public lobbies (host, language, mode, time limit, players, status), refreshes every 5 s, filters by text language and joins one in a click.
- Lobby management: three visibilities (public, code, private), invite links bound to their first visitor's address, host-set seats (2 to 30), spectators, kicking, closing the lobby, and 10 code attempts per minute per address.
- Guests: visitors pick a nickname on `/guest` before joining a lobby (by code, link, browser or quick play); they cannot host or use the dojo, have no profile, and the header shows an avatar generated from their name.
- Header nav: Home, Phantom Thieves and Leaderboards (races, training and lobbies start from the home page).
- Character files (`/fr/characters/[id]`, `/en/characters/[id]`; `/characters` opens the player's own thief): codename, real name, short bio, Persona, portrait and idle sprite for each of the 12 thieves, previous/next arrows and a roster. Signed-in players can pick their thief there. The art is the 320px all-out portrait until larger character art exists.
- Compendium (`/fr/compendium`, `/en/compendium`, in the main nav): the 12 confidant cards, face down until the player races as that thief, with progress (arcana turned over, MAX cards). Opening a card shows it big; it turns over to the stats with that thief (races, wins, best and average WPM, accuracy) and can be played to choose the thief. Achievement cards, rewards per rank and showing a card in lobbies are only ideas, not built.
- Language switcher (FR/EN) in the header: links to the same page in the other language and saves the choice in a `locale` cookie; `/` goes to the saved language, else the browser language, else French. On narrow screens only the other language shows.
- Leaderboard (`/fr/leaderboard`, `/en/leaderboard`): top 100 registered players by record WPM, with their profile picture, average WPM, accuracy and race count; 10 per page with previous/next arrows, each page loaded from the server; the signed-in player's row is highlighted.
- Race history: when a race ends, the server saves each registered player's place, WPM, accuracy and time in `race_results`, plus the full results (every racer's WPM series included). The results page charts every racer's WPM and flags a new personal best for registered players. The profile page (`/fr/profile`, `/en/profile`, registered users only: avatar, username, member since, character picker (Joker, Mona, Panther, Skull, Fox, Queen, Oracle, Noir, Crow, Violet, Black Mask or Sophia, each card showing the character's Persona; a change shows at once on their card in their current lobby and on the track from the next race), sign-in methods) shows races, wins, record, average WPM, accuracy, a WPM chart of the last 50 races and the paginated history (10 per page), where each race opens its full results again (`/profile/races/[id]`); the home dossier shows record, accuracy and race count, and links to the profile. Guests keep their races in `sessionStorage` (the results page writes them), so they last until the tab is closed; the home dossier shows them.

- Profile editing (`/fr/profile`, `/en/profile`): registered players change their display name (same rules as sign-up, unique) and upload a profile picture (JPEG, PNG or WebP, 2 MB max, real type checked on the server, stored in the database, resized for display). The new name shows at once in their current lobby; the picture shows on the profile, the home dossier and the header.

- Database foundation: migration runner, `users` / `oauth_accounts` / `sessions` schema, password hashing, session tokens, account validation and data-access functions (`lib/users.ts`). Used by email/password auth.

- Email/password sign up (`/fr/sign-up`, `/en/sign-up`), sign in (`/…/sign-in`), sign out, session cookie and current-user lookup. The header shows the signed-in username or Sign in / Join links.
- OAuth sign-in with Google, GitHub and Discord (buttons appear once the provider's credentials are in `.env`). Redirects use `APP_URL`, since behind Railway's proxy the request origin is the internal host.
- Favicon and Apple touch icon (`src/app/icon.png`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`) from the T//S logo.

## Mocked / not wired

- Server data comes from `src/mocks/player.ts`.
- The invite observer button does nothing and the spectator list is empty. Player cards show `-- WPM` as best speed until stats are stored.
- "Start a race" opens a new lobby to invite players into; matchmaking is only for quick 1v1.
- Key-audio selection is UI only (no key sounds yet).
- Avatar and in-page logo are placeholders (icon + wordmark) until real assets exist.

- Race texts are a small built-in list per language, with a separate list with digits (`lib/race-texts.ts`); countdown (3 s), grace period (30 s) and speed limit (30 keys/s) are fixed defaults in `lib/race-engine.ts`. Punctuation is always on.
- Full results (podium, chart, heatmap) live in memory with the lobby and disappear when it closes or the server restarts; only each player's summary is saved (see Built). Heatmap thresholds (100 ms fast, 250 ms or a miss = slow) are placeholders.
- Normal mode: wrong characters stay and must be deleted; corrected mistakes still count against accuracy.
